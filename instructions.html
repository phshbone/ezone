BETA TESTER GUIDE

E-Zone Setup

Tap your device type below for step-by-step instructions.

🍎iPHONE
🤖ANDROID
1
Add E-Zone to Your Home Screen
⚠️ Must use Safari — Chrome won't give you the Add to Home Screen option.
1
Open the E-Zone link in Safari
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (box with arrow pointing up)
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
4
Name it E-Zone and tap Add
5
The icon now lives on your home screen like a regular app ✓
2
Saving Your Photos
1
After taking a photo, tap Continue in the preview screen
2
Your iPhone Share Sheet will appear automatically
3
Tap Save Image
4
Photo saves to your Camera Roll in the Photos app — look in Recents
💡 Each photo has a timestamp label so they're easy to find and identify later.
3
Finding & Emailing Your Results
1
Open your Photos app and go to Recents — your E-Zone photos will be there with timestamp labels
2
The app will open your Mail app pre-filled with the correct email address and subject line — don't change those
3
In the body of the email, type your location first (example: Troy Hill School, Morris County)
4
After typing your location, tap the paperclip icon — or press and hold in the body and tap Insert Photo or Video
5
Select your E-Zone photos from Camera Roll → Recents
6
Hit Send ✓
📍 Always type your location in the email body first — before attaching anything. This is how we know where each incident happened.
1
Add E-Zone to Your Home Screen
✓ Use Chrome on Android for the best experience.
1
Open the E-Zone link in Chrome
2
Tap the three dots in the top right corner
3
Tap Add to Home Screen
4
Name it E-Zone and tap Add
5
Tap Add again if a second prompt appears
6
The icon now lives on your home screen like a regular app ✓
2
Saving Your Photos
1
After taking a photo, tap Continue in the preview screen
2
A save or share option will appear — tap Save or Download
3
Photo saves to your Downloads folder or Gallery depending on your phone
💡 Each photo has a timestamp label so they're easy to find and identify later.
3
Finding & Emailing Your Results
1
Open your Gallery app and check Recents or Downloads album — or open Files → Downloads
2
The app will open your email app pre-filled with the correct address and subject line — don't change those
3
In the body of the email, type your location first (example: Troy Hill School, Morris County)
4
After typing your location, tap the paperclip icon in the toolbar above your keyboard
5
Navigate to Downloads or Gallery and select your E-Zone photos
6
Hit Send ✓
📍 Always type your location in the email body first — before attaching anything. This is how we know where each incident happened.
